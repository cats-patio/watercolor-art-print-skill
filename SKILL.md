# Photo to Watercolor Art Print Skill

## Core Vision & Orientation Rules
- **Primary Goal**: Transform the input photograph into an authentic hand-painted watercolor illustration.
- **Orientation & Aspect Ratio**: Strictly preserve the original input photo's orientation and aspect ratio. If the input image is landscape (horizontal), output a landscape watercolor print; if portrait (vertical), output a portrait watercolor print.
- **Medium Style**: Cold-press watercolor paper stock, soft color bleeding, translucent washes, visible paper grain, gentle natural lighting.
- **Framing**: Clean edge, subtle natural white margin around the painting.

## Absolute Exclusion Principles (Hard Constraints)
- **NO TEXT**: Absolutely zero typography, zero titles, zero captions, zero greetings, zero watermarks.
- **NO GRAPHICS**: Zero stamps, zero postmarks, zero ink seals, zero lines, zero postcard layouts.
- **NO CROP ALTERATION**: Do not rotate, flip, or force a landscape image into a vertical frame.

## Direct Prompt Template
A pure, minimalist hand-painted watercolor painting based directly on this input photograph. Rendered in a realistic wet-on-wet watercolor style with soft translucent washes, visible cold-press paper grain, and natural color bleeding. Strictly preserve the original aspect ratio and orientation (horizontal/landscape for horizontal input, vertical/portrait for vertical input). The painting features a clean, narrow white paper margin with subtle natural watercolor edges. 100% pure illustration only, completely text-free, no graphics, no stamps, no postmarks, no logos, no titles, no decorative overlays.

## Guidelines
- **Best Input**: Landscapes, architecture, street scenes, outdoor scenic views.
- **Avoid**: Extremely close-up human faces.
