# Vintage Watercolor Postcard Skill

## Prompt Template
Generate a watercolor painting of this image, soft natural color bleeding, visible brush strokes, wet-on-wet technique, translucent watercolor washes, delicate paper texture, gentle pastel colors, hand-painted fine art illustration, soft dreamy lighting. Presented as a vintage travel postcard with a white border frame, postcard style layout, no stamps, no postmarks, no text overlays, keep the original background color unchanged, only add the white border frame.

## Guidelines
- **Best Input**: Landscape photos, city streets, architecture.
- **Avoid**: Close-up portraits or human faces.
